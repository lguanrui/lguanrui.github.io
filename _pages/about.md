My name is Guanrui Li (Chinese characters: 李冠睿), pronounced "Guan (冠) Ray (睿) Li (李)". You can call me Rui (Ray) for short.

I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Robotics Engineering](https://www.wpi.edu/academics/departments/robotics-engineering) at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI), with an affiliated appointment in the Department of Mechanical and Materials Engineering. I direct the [Aerial-robot Control and Perception (ACP) Lab](https://acp-lab.wpi.edu/). I also serve as Associate Co-Chair of the IEEE RAS Technical Committee on Aerial Robotics and Unmanned Aerial Vehicles, as an Associate Editor for IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L), IROS, and ICUAS, and as an Area Chair for Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS) 2027.

## Research

My research develops the planning, control, perception, and learning that let flying robots navigate the world, interact with it physically, and collaborate with other robots and people. At the ACP Lab we organize this work around three pillars:

- [**Aerial robot agility, intelligence and safety**](https://acp-lab.wpi.edu/research/agility-intelligence-safety/): fast planning and control, robust perception and estimation, learning-based navigation, and safety guarantees that let aerial robots fly aggressively and reliably in complex environments.
- [**Aerial physical intelligence**](https://acp-lab.wpi.edu/research/aerial-physical-intelligence/): aerial robots that carry, manipulate, and make contact with the world, from cable-suspended payload transport and perching to tactile control and physical collaboration with people.
- [**Multi-robot collaboration**](https://acp-lab.wpi.edu/research/multi-robot-collaboration/): teams of aerial robots that cooperatively transport and manipulate objects, self-assemble into modular structures, avoid each other safely, and work alongside humans.

Our work is supported by the National Science Foundation and Amazon Robotics. See the [lab website](https://acp-lab.wpi.edu/) for our [people](https://acp-lab.wpi.edu/people/), [flight videos](https://www.youtube.com/@ACPLab-wpi), and [news](https://acp-lab.wpi.edu/post/). If you are interested in joining the lab as a PhD student, postdoc, or WPI MS/undergraduate researcher, please read the [Join Us](https://acp-lab.wpi.edu/join/) page first.

## Background

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from New York University, advised by [Prof. Giuseppe Loianno](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Faculty/Homepages/loiannog.html) (now at UC Berkeley) in the [Agile Robotics and Perception Lab (ARPL)](https://wp.nyu.edu/arpl/), and stayed on as a postdoctoral researcher at ARPL before joining WPI in August 2024. Before NYU, I was a research associate in the [AirLab](https://theairlab.org/) at Carnegie Mellon University's Robotics Institute with Prof. Sebastian Scherer, and a research assistant in Prof. Mark Yim's ModLab at the University of Pennsylvania. I earned my master's degree in Robotics from the University of Pennsylvania, where I worked with Prof. Vijay Kumar and Prof. Mark Yim at the GRASP Lab, and my bachelor's degree in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics from Sun Yat-sen University, where I was recognized as an Honors Undergraduate.

My work has been recognized with the 2024 NYU University-Wide Outstanding Dissertation Award, the 2024 Alexander Hessel Award for the Best Ph.D. Dissertation in Electrical Engineering, selection as an NSF CPS Rising Star in 2023, the 2022 Dante Youla Award for Graduate Research Excellence at NYU Tandon, and an Outstanding Deployed Systems Paper Award finalist at IEEE ICRA 2022. My research has been featured by IEEE Spectrum and the Discovery Channel.

You can find my [publications](/publications/) and [CV](/files/cv_guanrui.pdf) on this site.
